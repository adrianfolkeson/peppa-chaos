## 📋 LEVELS

### LEVEL 1: BEDROOM DISASTER 🌙
- Location: Bedroom
- Duration: 2-3 min
- Difficulty: Easy (Tutorial)
- Objectives: Break toys, spill juice, draw on walls, flood bathroom
- Enemy: Jennifer Pig
- Escape: Under bed / closet

### LEVEL 2: SCHOOL CHAOS 🏫
- Location: School
- Duration: 3-4 min
- Difficulty: Medium
- Boss: Teacher Pig
- Objectives: Skip class, pull alarm, hack speakers, release frogs

### LEVEL 3: BEACH DAY GONE WRONG 🏖️
- Location: Beach
- Duration: 4-5 min
- Difficulty: Medium-Hard
- Final Event: Black hole opens
- Escape: Run up beach

### LEVEL 4: MALL DISASTER 🛒
- Location: Mall
- Duration: 4-5 min
- Difficulty: Hard
- Enemies: Security guards + Jennifer
- Escape: Through vents

### LEVEL 5: NEIGHBORHOOD 🔥
- Location: Open neighborhood
- Duration: 5-6 min
- Difficulty: Hard
- Open sections with multiple paths
- Random events

### LEVEL 6: BLACK HOLE ENDING 🌀
- Location: Reality collapsing
- Duration: 5-7 min
- Difficulty: EXTREME
- Boss: Mummy Pig (corrupted)
- 3 Endings: Good, Bad, Secret
